1. Download
Download bn.apk

Your browser may warn that this file type can harm your device. Tap Download anyway (or Keep). bn is not on the Google Play Store, so every phone shows this warning.

2. Install
  1. Open the downloaded bn.apk (from your browser's downloads or the Files app).
  2. If Android says your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Google Play Protect says the app is blocked or unsafe, tap More details, then Install anyway. This appears because the app is not from the Play Store and Google has not scanned it.
  4. No "Install anyway" button? Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect, then the settings gear, and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. Install bn, then turn it back on.

Updating? Install the new file straight over the old one. Do not uninstall bn first, or you lose your credits.

3. Set up the phone
  1. Open bn. A card called Finish setup on the first screen lists what is left. Work down it, tapping the button on each line.
  2. Allow permissions: allow SMS, Phone and Notifications. If SMS is greyed out or blocked, go to Settings > Apps > bn, tap the menu at the top right, choose Allow restricted settings, then allow SMS again.
  3. Turn on the dialing helper: tap Turn on, find bn USSD helper in the list and switch it on. Bundle codes must be dialed one step at a time, and this helper does the typing. It does nothing except while bn is dialing a sale. If it is greyed out, use Allow restricted settings as above.
  4. Choose your M-Pesa line: if your phone has two SIMs, tap Choose and pick the SIM your customers pay into.
  5. Set battery use for bn to No restrictions (Settings > Apps > bn > Battery). Keep the phone plugged in, with the SIM inside and no screen lock, so the dialing menus can appear.
4. Add credits
  1. In bn, under ADD CREDITS, tap COPY next to your Device ID.
  2. Send the Device ID to us with your payment.
  3. We reply with a token that starts with BN$V$. Copy the whole message.
  4. Open bn, tap PASTE, then ADD CREDITS.

1 credit = Ksh 1 of sales. A Ksh 19 sale uses 19 credits. When credits run out, sales are not dialed until you add more.
